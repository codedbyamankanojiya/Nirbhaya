# 🤝 Code of Conduct — Nirbhaya

Nirbhaya is an independent, life-critical personal safety platform conceived, architected, and developed individually by **Aman Kanojiya**. 

As the sole creator, developer, and maintainer of this project, I am personally committed to ensuring that participation in this repository, community discussions, and issue tracking is a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

---

## 🙋‍♂️ My Pledge

I pledge that anyone participating in or contributing to this project will be treated with dignity and respect, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

I expect everyone who interacts with this project to act in ways that foster an open, constructive, welcoming, and safe environment.

---

## 🎯 Community Standards

Behavior that contributes to a positive, healthy environment includes:

* Demonstrating empathy, patience, and kindness toward others
* Respecting differing viewpoints, constructive feedback, and technical ideas
* Focusing on user safety and the life-saving mission of this application
* Communicating with professional courtesy and humility

Unacceptable behavior includes:

* The use of sexualized language, imagery, or unwelcome advances
* Trolling, insulting, demeaning, or derogatory remarks
* Personal, political, or ad hominem attacks
* Public or private harassment of any kind
* Doxxing or publishing others' private information without their explicit consent
* Any conduct harmful to the project or disrespectful to others

---

## 🛡️ Sole Maintainer Responsibilities

As the sole author and maintainer of Nirbhaya:
1. I (**Aman Kanojiya**) hold sole responsibility for clarifying and upholding these standards.
2. I reserve the right to review, edit, reject, or remove any comments, commits, pull requests, issues, or discussions that fail to uphold this Code of Conduct.
3. I take all reports seriously and will take prompt, fair action against any unacceptable or abusive behavior.
